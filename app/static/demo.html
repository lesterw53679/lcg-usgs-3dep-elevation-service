How line sampling works

The browser measures the complete multi-vertex line geodesically, selects a readable spacing from 25 meters upward, includes both endpoints, and caps the request at 200 samples. Longer lines are intentionally sampled more sparsely.

What the 3D map means

Tilt, rotation, hillshade, and vertical exaggeration help reveal terrain. They are visualization controls. The numerical table is the service result and remains unchanged when the display exaggeration changes.
